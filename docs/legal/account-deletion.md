# Delete your RunLime account

You can delete your account at any time.

**In the app:** Profile → Delete account → confirm.

**On the web:** sign in on this page with the same email, Google or Apple account, then choose **Delete my account**.

### What is deleted
Your account, profile, date of birth, emergency contact, weight, follows and blocks, club memberships, RSVPs, check-ins, runs and any community times you shared. This happens immediately and can't be undone.

### What we keep
- Event totals (for example "120 attending") stay, but they are no longer linked to you.
- Organiser payment records and invoices are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it. The link to your account is removed.
- Our audit log of administrator and organiser actions contains no personal fields. Once your account is deleted, entries can no longer be linked to you.

### Subscriptions
Deleting your account **doesn't cancel** a Premium subscription bought through Apple or Google. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
