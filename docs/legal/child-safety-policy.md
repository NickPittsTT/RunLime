# RunLime Child Safety Policy (CSAE Standards)

RunLime has **zero tolerance** for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, trafficking, and any content or behaviour that sexualises or endangers children.

## Our standards
1. **Adults only.** RunLime accounts are for people aged 18 and over.
   - An age gate runs before sign-in, and our servers enforce the rule again.
   - Children don't have accounts. They attend events only with an adult, who records them as a **number** in their group. No child's name, photo or details are collected.
2. **Prohibited content and conduct.** Any CSAE content or behaviour is forbidden anywhere in RunLime: nicknames, community times, reports, organiser event information, and any other content.
3. **Reporting in the app.** Every profile, community time and event has a **Report** option, including a "child safety" reason. These reports are prioritised.
4. **Action.** When we become aware of CSAM or CSAE:
   - we remove the content
   - we suspend the account(s) involved
   - we preserve the evidence securely
   - we report to the appropriate authorities, as described below
5. **Reporting to authorities.** We report confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) through its CyberTipline, and to the appropriate authorities in Trinidad and Tobago:
   - the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service, Child Protection Unit
   - the Children's Authority of Trinidad and Tobago
6. **Compliance.** We comply with applicable child-safety laws, including the Children Act of Trinidad and Tobago.
