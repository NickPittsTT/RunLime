# Community Times

{{community_disclaimer}}

- Sharing a time is optional. You can share one result per event, and only for an event you checked in to.
- Each time is labelled **Tracked** (recorded with the RunLime tracker) or **Typed in** (entered by hand).
- Times faster than a realistic limit are rejected automatically.
- You can withdraw your shared time at any time.
- Anyone can report a time. You can block other users.
- There are no prizes, rewards or official rankings for community times.
