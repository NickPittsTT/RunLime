# Health Disclaimer

- RunLime is a fitness and event app. **It isn't a medical device and doesn't give medical advice.**
- Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise programme, especially if you have a heart condition, high blood pressure, diabetes, asthma, are pregnant, or have any other health concern.
- **Distance, pace, splits, steps and calories are estimates.** They depend on your phone's GPS and motion sensors, and may be inaccurate.
  - Calories are estimated from your weight (if you choose to add it), your pace and published activity values. They aren't a measurement.
- Stop running and get help if you feel chest pain, faintness, severe breathlessness or dizziness.
- Trinidad and Tobago is hot and humid. Hydrate, use sun protection, and avoid running in extreme heat.
- Stay aware of traffic. Follow marshal instructions and the law. Don't look at your phone while crossing roads.
- Emergency: call **990** (ambulance) or **999** (police).
