# RunLime Moderation Policy

RunLime is a friendly lime for runners. This policy explains what's allowed and what we do when something isn't.

## What can be posted
Users can post a display name, a nickname and community times. Users can also report content and other users. Organisers post event information. All of it must be accurate, respectful and lawful.

## Not allowed
- Harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying
- Sexual content of any kind, and any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children (see the [Child Safety Policy](/legal/child-safety)). Zero tolerance
- Impersonation, false or misleading event information, spam or scams
- Offensive or misleading nicknames
- Times that are impossible or clearly false (we also automatically reject paces faster than the configured limit)
- Personal information about other people
- Attempts to get around blocks, suspensions or the 18+ rule

## Your tools
- **Report** any community time, profile or event from the ⋯ menu.
- **Block** any user. They can no longer follow you, and you won't see each other's content.
- **Remove followers** or switch your account to Private at any time.

## What we do
1. Every report goes to a moderation queue that RunLime administrators review. We aim to review reports **within 24 hours** and prioritise any child-safety reports.
2. Possible actions: no action, removal of content, a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent account closure. Serious or repeated breaches lead to closure.
3. If content may break the law, we preserve the relevant records and report to the authorities where required.
4. Every moderation action is recorded in our audit log.

## Appeals
If you think we got it wrong, email {{support_email}} within 30 days. Include your account email and what happened. A different administrator will review the decision where possible.
