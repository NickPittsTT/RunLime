# RunLime Moderation Policy

RunLime is a friendly lime for runners. This policy explains what's allowed and what we do when something isn't.

## What can be posted
Runners can post a display name, a nickname, a profile photo, a runner level and community times, and can ask for a new club to be added. Club leaders approve who joins their club. Organisers post event information and can send short updates about a race to the people going to it (at most the number set by RunLime per race per week, currently 2). Everything must be accurate, respectful and lawful.

## Profile photos
- Use a photo of yourself, or an image that doesn't identify anyone else (for example a logo or a running shoe).
- No nudity or sexual content, no violence, hate symbols, drugs or weapons, no text with contact details or advertising.
- No photos where a child is the main subject. RunLime is for adults.
- No photos of other people without their permission.
- We remove location and other hidden details from photos when you upload them.

## Not allowed
- Harassment, threats, hate speech or bullying
- Sexual content of any kind, and any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers children (see the [Child Safety Policy](/legal/child-safety)). Zero tolerance
- Impersonation, false or misleading event information, spam or scams
- Offensive or misleading nicknames, photos or club names
- Organiser messages that are advertising, unrelated to the race, or sent to get around the limit
- Times that are impossible or clearly false (we also automatically reject paces faster than the configured limit)
- Personal information about other people
- Attempts to get around blocks, suspensions or the 18+ rule

## Your tools
- **Report** a profile photo, profile, club, community time or race listing with the **Report** button next to it.
- **Report a safety concern** at any time from Help & legal or your Profile. Choose "A child may be at risk" for anything that could harm a child.
- **Block** any user. They can no longer follow you, and you won't see each other's content.
- **Remove followers** or switch your account to Private at any time.
- The person you report is never told who reported them.

## What we do
1. Every report goes to a moderation queue that RunLime administrators review. We aim to review every report **within 48 hours**. **Child-safety reports are always handled first**: they go to the top of the queue, ahead of every other report, and content that may put a child at risk is removed as soon as an administrator confirms it.
2. Child sexual abuse material: we remove it, keep the records the law requires, and report it to the Trinidad and Tobago Police Service and to the US National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (CyberTipline). We never share such material except with the authorities as required.
3. Possible actions: no action, removal of content (for example a photo), removal of a club leader's role, a warning, temporary suspension, or permanent account closure. Serious or repeated breaches lead to closure.
4. If content may break the law, we preserve the relevant records and report to the authorities where required.
5. Every moderation action is recorded in our audit log.
