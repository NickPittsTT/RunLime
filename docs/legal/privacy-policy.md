# RunLime Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 15 October 2026 (draft)

RunLime is a calendar for 5K and similar road races in Trinidad and Tobago, with tools to RSVP, check in on race day, track your own runs and follow other runners. RunLime is operated by {{operator_name}} ("RunLime", "we", "us"). Contact us at {{support_email}}.

We follow the General Privacy Principles of the Trinidad and Tobago Data Protection Act. We collect the minimum we need, keep your account private by default, and ask for your consent before using location, health-related data, or sharing anything publicly.

## 1. Who can use RunLime
RunLime accounts are only for adults aged **18 and over**. When you first open the app we ask for your date of birth before you choose how to sign in. If you are under 18, the app stops and nothing is sent to us. Children can come to events only with an adult. The adult can add them to their group as a **number** (we never collect children's names or other details).

## 2. What we collect and why

| Data | When | Why | Legal basis / choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Email address, or your Google/Apple account identifier | Sign-in | To create and secure your account | Needed for the service |
| Date of birth | Sign-up | To confirm you are 18+ | Needed for the service; it can't be changed in the app |
| Display name, optional nickname, home area | Sign-up / profile | To show your profile and suggest nearby races | Needed for the service |
| Account privacy setting (Private by default, or Public) | Settings | To control who can see your race passport and the races you are attending | Your choice |
| Consent records (terms, privacy, location, health data, public sharing) | When you agree | To show what you agreed to and when | Legal obligation / your consent |
| Emergency contact (optional) | Profile | So you have it on hand on race day | Your choice; it's encrypted before it is stored |
| Weight (optional) | Health data settings | To estimate calories burned | Your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Followers, follow requests, blocks | When you use social features | To run followers and safety tools | Needed for those features |
| Club memberships | When you join a club | To show club activity you opted into | Your choice |

| Community times you choose to share | When you share a result | To show it on that race's community board | Your choice; you can withdraw it at any time |
| Reports you make and moderation records | When content is reported | To keep the community safe | Legitimate interest / legal obligation |

| Organiser details (organisation name, contact email and phone, website, description) and payment records for listings | If you apply as an organiser | To verify organisers and invoice listings | Contract / legal obligation (tax records) |
| Crash reports and privacy-respecting usage analytics (no advertising IDs, no session recordings) | While using the app | To fix bugs and improve RunLime | Legitimate interest; you can opt out in Settings |

**Location.** We ask for location only "while using the app". If you start a run, tracking continues with the screen off and a visible notification (Android) or location indicator (iOS) for as long as the run lasts. We never track you in the background at any other time.

**What we don't do:** we don't sell your data, we don't show ads, we don't use your data for advertising, and we don't share your precise location with other users.

## 3. Who can see what
- **Everyone:** your display name or nickname, and any community time you choose to share. On public accounts, also your race passport and the races you are attending.
- **Your approved followers** (private accounts): your race passport (races attended, total km) and the races you are attending.
- **Organisers:** totals and trends for their own events, plus nicknames of attendees who opted in. They never see your email, date of birth or contact details.
- **Our administrators:** only what they need to run and moderate the service. They can't read your emergency contact. Every administrator action is recorded in an audit log.
- Your runs, routes, times and history are **private** unless you share a result.

## 4. Service providers
We use trusted providers that process data on our behalf under contract:
- Supabase (database, authentication, file storage). **Your data is stored in {{hosting_region}}.**
- Cloudflare (website hosting)
- Expo (push notification delivery)
- Apple and Google (sign-in, app stores, in-app purchases, maps)
- RevenueCat (subscription status)
- Resend (sign-in emails)
- Sentry (crash reports)
- PostHog (privacy-respecting analytics)

This means your data is transferred to and stored outside Trinidad and Tobago, mainly in the United States. Where it is, we rely on our providers' contractual safeguards.

## 5. How long we keep data
| Data | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Incomplete sign-ups (age gate not finished) | Deleted automatically within about 1 hour |
| Runs, RSVPs, check-ins, follows | Until you delete them or your account |
| Shared community times | Until you withdraw them or delete your account |
| Push delivery records | 90 days |
| Moderation records | Up to 2 years after the case closes |
| Organiser payment records and invoices | 7 years (tax law), with the person detached after account deletion |
| Audit log of administrator and organiser actions | Kept for accountability. It never contains personal fields, and entries become unlinkable to you once your account is deleted |

## 6. Your rights and choices
- **See and download your data:** Profile → Your data → Download (in the app or on the web).
- **Correct your data:** edit your profile. To correct your date of birth, contact support.
- **Withdraw consent:** turn off location, health data, notifications or public sharing in Settings at any time.
- **Delete your account:** in the app (Profile → Delete account) or at {{website}}/delete-account. Deletion is immediate and permanent, apart from the records we must keep by law (section 5).
- **Complain:** contact us first at {{support_email}}. You may also contact the Office of the Information Commissioner of Trinidad and Tobago once that office is operating.

## 7. Security
Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Your emergency contact is also encrypted with a separate key before it is stored. Access is controlled by row-level security in our database. Administrators must use two-factor authentication.

## 8. Changes
If we make material changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take effect. The version and date are shown at the top of this page.
