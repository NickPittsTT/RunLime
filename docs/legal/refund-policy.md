# RunLime Refund Policy

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026 (draft)

This policy explains when RunLime gives refunds. RunLime is operated by {{operator_name}}. Contact us at {{support_email}}.

RunLime does not take race entry fees. If you paid to enter a race, ask that race's organiser about refunds.

## 1. Organiser listing fees (paid by bank transfer)

Organisers pay for event listings through the RunLime web portal, by bank transfer only.

### When you get a refund

| Situation | Refund |
|---|---|
| **We reject your listing** (it is not approved) | Full refund of the listing fee |
| **You cancel your race** before race day, and record the cancellation in the organiser portal | Full refund of the listing fee for that race |
| **You paid twice, or paid more than the invoice** | Refund of the duplicate payment or the extra amount |

### When there is no refund
- **Your race takes place.** The listing fee pays for the listing, whatever the turnout.
- **Your race is postponed.** Your listing moves to the new date at no extra cost. If you later cancel the race, the cancellation row above applies.
- **We remove your listing because you broke our Terms of Use** (for example, false or misleading event information).
- **Free listings** (such as free Basic listings for registered charities) have nothing to refund.

### How we pay refunds
- By **bank transfer**, in Trinidad and Tobago dollars (TTD), for the **full amount** you paid for the item being refunded. We do not deduct an administration fee.
- To the **bank account the payment came from**, unless you ask in writing for a different account in your organisation's name. We will confirm the account details with you by email before sending. We never ask for passwords or card details.
- Rejections and cancellations recorded in the portal start a refund automatically. For a duplicate payment, email {{support_email}} with the payment date and amount.

### Timing
We send the refund within **{{refund_processing_days}} business days** of the day the refund is confirmed (the day we reject the listing, the day you record the cancellation, or the day we match a duplicate payment to our bank records). Your bank may take longer to show the money.

## 2. RunLime Premium (bought in the app)

Premium is sold only through the **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**. Apple and Google take the payment, so **refunds follow Apple's and Google's rules**, and only they can issue them. We cannot refund store purchases ourselves.
- **Apple:** request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
- **Google Play:** request a refund from your Google Play order history, or through the Google Play Help Center.

You can cancel Premium at any time in your store account settings. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

## 3. Questions
Email {{support_email}}. This policy does not take away any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of Trinidad and Tobago.
