# RunLime Support

We're here to help.

- **Email:** {{support_email}}. We aim to reply within 2 working days.
- **Report a safety concern:** use **Report** in the app. Child-safety concerns: {{child_safety_contact_email}}.
- **Missing race?** Use "Suggest a race" in the app.
- **Organisers:** apply and manage your events on the web portal at {{website}}/organiser.

### Common questions
**Who can use RunLime?** Adults aged 18 and over. Children can come to races with an adult, who adds them to their group.

**Does RunLime handle race registration or official results?** No. Register and get official results from the race organiser.

**How do I delete my account?** Profile → Delete account in the app, or {{website}}/delete-account.

**How do I cancel Premium?** In your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
